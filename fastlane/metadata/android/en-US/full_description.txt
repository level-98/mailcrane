Mailcrane is a free, open-source email app. It works with your existing email account, keeps your mail on your phone, and never sends your mail or your data to anyone else.

Works with your provider
• IMAP and SMTP for almost every provider, set up automatically from your address in most cases, or by hand.
• JMAP for providers that offer it, such as Fastmail and Stalwart.
• Several accounts, each in its own colour, and one inbox for all of them.

Private by default
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours: the app talks only to your own mail provider.
• Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked until you allow them, and the app tells you which trackers it removed.
• Tracking parameters are removed from links before they open.
• Before a link opens, you see where it really goes, with a warning when the text names a different site, the address mixes alphabets, or it isn't secure.
• A "Verified" or "Failed" mark shows what your provider found when it checked who sent a message.
• App lock with your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Everything you need
• Conversations, full-text search that works offline, and search filters.
• Reply, reply all, forward, attachments, rich text and signatures.
• Drafts saved as you type, an outbox that sends when you're back online, scheduled send and undo send.
• Snooze, swipe actions, bulk actions, rules (also run by your server where it supports ManageSieve), and one-tap unsubscribe.
• Create, rename, move and delete folders.
• Recent messages downloaded in the background, so they open at once, even offline.
• New-mail notifications, a home-screen widget, dark mode with dynamic colour, and layouts for tablets.

Free software
Mailcrane is licensed under the MPL-2.0 and built in the open. Its engine is written in Rust and shared with the iOS app to come.

Mailcrane is still young: please report problems on the project's issue tracker.
