Fastmail, Stalwart and other JMAP servers work with Mailcrane. In manual setup, choose JMAP and enter your domain or the server’s session URL (see [Add an account](@/docs/add-account.md)). You can read, organise and send mail, and manage folders, like on any other account.

The implementation and provider coverage are tracked in the [M3 roadmap issues](https://github.com/mailcrane/mailcrane/issues?q=is%3Aissue+M3-04). Step-by-step instructions for each JMAP provider will follow once they’ve been tested with real accounts.
