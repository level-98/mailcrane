Mailcrane is a private, open-source email app for Android with a Rust core. It needs Android 8.0 or later and an email account that offers IMAP and SMTP, or JMAP.

1. **Install it** from the [download page](@/download.md). Mailcrane isn’t in the stores yet: the first public release is being prepared, and the page shows how to get notified when it’s out. For testers, the same page has an unofficial nightly test build. It isn’t for daily use, or for mail you can’t afford to lose.
2. **Add your account.** The first time you open the app, tap **Add account** and follow [Add an account](@/docs/add-account.md).
3. **Look around.** The [features page](@/features.md) lists what the app does, and [what’s coming](@/roadmap.md) shows what’s being built.

Install Mailcrane only from the links on the download page, and never share your password or private mail in a GitHub issue.
