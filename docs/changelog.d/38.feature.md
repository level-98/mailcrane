Send now always works, even offline: messages wait in an Outbox and are sent as soon as the network or server is back, retrying after 30 seconds, then 1, 2 and 5 minutes, then every 15 minutes for up to a day. Messages the server refuses for good show the reason, and can be retried, edited or deleted from the new Outbox entry in the folder drawer. Sending keeps going in the background. A message caught mid-send by a closed app is checked against the Sent folder before it is sent again.
