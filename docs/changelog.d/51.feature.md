Search: tap the search icon above the message list to search your mail instantly, even offline, across subjects, people and downloaded message text. Accents do not matter ("élodie" finds "Elodie"). Filters work too: from:, to:, subject:, "exact phrases", has:attachment, is:unread, is:flagged, before:/after:YYYY-MM-DD and in:folder. Pressing search also asks the server, so messages that were never downloaded are found. Recent searches are remembered on the device.
