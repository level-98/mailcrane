Sign in with Google for Gmail and Google Workspace accounts: setup opens Google's sign-in page in your browser (Mailcrane never sees your Google password), then reads and sends mail with the access token (XOAUTH2). The refresh token is kept in the system's secure storage. Gmail labels show as folders; archiving moves a message to All Mail (removing its Inbox label), deleting moves it to Trash, and a flag is Gmail's star.
